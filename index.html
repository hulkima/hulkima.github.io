Haokai Ma 

Postdoctoral Research Fellow

NExT++ Research Center / NCL

National University of Singapore

Email: haokai.ma1997@gmail.com
I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in National University of Singapore (NUS), where I am a member of NExT++ Research Center, supervised by Prof. Chua Tat-Seng. I am also under National Cybersecurity R&D Lab, where I work with Prof. Chang Ee-Chien. Before that, I had received my Ph.D. degree in Shandong University in June 2024, supervised by Prof. Meng Lei. My research interests span Recommender System, the safety and security issues within LLM, and the application of LLMs in Cybersecurity. I have more than 30 publications appeared in several top conferences such as KDD, ICLR, AAAI, IJCAI, MM and Recsys, and journals including TOIS, CVMJ and Journal of Software. I have been invited as the reviewer for NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, ACL, AAAI, TKDE and TOIS, and the Regular PC for Recsys Reproducibility track and Demo track. Moreover, I serve as the Student Volunteer Co-chair in ICAIF 2025 and the Session Chair in IJCAI and IJCNN.

I am looking for highly motivated PhD/master/undergraduate students to collaborate on various Recommendation scenarios, the safety and security issues within LLMs (Prompt Injection, MAS/GUI Agent Security), as well as on the application of LLMs in Cybersecurity (CTI Report Understanding and Confidence Faithfulness in High-stakes Tasks). If you have interest, please feel free to send your CV to haokai.ma1997@gmail.com. All CVs will be carefully treated!
